How I work

Four things I do in the same order every time.

01

Start with the operational reality

The data arrives from someone else's system in a shape nobody designed. The Egypt advisor export was a supervisor's Daxium file whose layout changed month to month. That is the starting condition, not an edge case to handle later.

02

Define what valid data means

The dedup key is a decision, not a detail: what makes two rows the same event rather than two events. Here it was advisor, date, store and shift, and that one definition sits underneath every number the app shows, so it gets settled before anything is calculated.

03

Build with the field team

The rollout was training, not a link. Onboarding and training covered roughly 20 field users into sustained daily use, on top of sales enablement workshops I built and delivered for a 30 plus person advisor team: the curriculum, the live sessions and the performance tracking. The tracker landed inside an existing training relationship, which is why it stuck.

04

Measure adoption, not delivery

Nobody was required to use the tracker. 18 advisors across Cairo, Sharm and Hurghada logged their own sales in it daily through the January to April window. A mandated tool only tells you people were told to open it.

18 live, about 20 onboarded. The January to April export holds 18 advisors. Onboarding and training covered roughly 20 field users, a figure that includes people who moved off the team inside the window. Both are real and they count different things.

Markets, not doors. The data holds 3 distinct store values at city level, so coverage is stated at market level: Cairo 12, Sharm 3, Hurghada 3. No door count is claimed.

Model

The rules a system has to encode

The UAE rental regulation model Homebase was built around: RERA bands, the 90 day notice rule, Ejari sequencing and the bilingual requirement. Pricing rules that will not display a saving unless it is real, because the generator prices against purchase price rather than a catalog page.

Model here means domain rules, not machine learning. No model has been shipped into production. The sales tracker and the move-out generator contain none at all, and Homebase's model path is opt in behind a flag. The honest AI signal is the evaluation work on Homebase, which is written up on the FAQ.
