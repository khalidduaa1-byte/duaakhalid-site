Your questions answered directly.

Written by me. These are the questions that actually come up when someone reads the site, including the uncomfortable ones about how small the numbers are and what the AI work actually is.

01. What did you actually build across these projects? +

Beauty Advisor Sales Tracker: A PWA for Dolce & Gabbana Beauty in travel retail MEA where 18 advisors log daily sales, backed by an ingest pipeline that de-duplicates exports before KPIs are calculated.

LVMH Sell-in Tool: Internal product owner for Givenchy & Kenzo sell-in decision support tool, cutting ordering time from 45 mins to 5 mins across 30+ locations.

Dolce & Gabbana Account Health: Benchmarking layer tracking a 13-market portfolio that flags at-risk accounts early.

Move-Out Sale Site: A Python generator producing a buyer-facing catalog and internal profit tracker from a single source list.

02. What is the dedup key and why does it matter? +

The dedup key defines what makes two rows the same event rather than two events. Here it was advisor + date + store + shift.

Duplicate rows double-count sales and inflate Days Worked, distorting every daily KPI. Settling this key upfront ensured that across 1,324 ingested rows, 29 duplicates were safely removed and zero rows were rejected.

03. Isn't 18 beauty advisors a small number? +

Yes, 18 is a small number and I am not going to dress it up. What matters is that nobody was required to use it.

The tracker was optional. All 18 advisors across Cairo, Sharm, and Hurghada logged into it daily because it solved a real friction point. Mandated tools show compliance; optional tools that teams open daily show real product adoption.

04. How much AI is actually in these projects? +

Less than the word usually implies, and I would rather say so directly than let it slide.

The Sales Tracker and Move-Out generator contain no ML/AI models. Homebase contains an opt-in model path behind a feature flag (USE_LLM_SCOPE=true), while its default path is a deterministic template builder.

The real AI work was evaluation: running structured usability sessions and analyzing voice agent call output logs to catch failure modes before production.
